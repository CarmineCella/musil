## Bundled data

`src/hrtf_kemar.csv` is the MIT KEMAR compact head-related impulse response set (Bill Gardner
and Keith Martin, MIT Media Laboratory, 1994), copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory, provided
free with no restrictions on use provided the authors are cited; converted from the C tables
of Aristotel Digenis's mit-hrtf-lib (MIT licence). If you use Musil's binaural rendering in
research or a product, cite: W. G. Gardner and K. D. Martin, "HRTF measurements of a KEMAR
dummy-head microphone", MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report #280, 1994.
